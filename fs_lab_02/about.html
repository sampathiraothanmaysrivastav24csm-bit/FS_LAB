About the Training & Placement Department

ANITS provides rigorous Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) starting in the pre-final year to prepare engineering students for software and core sector jobs. The in-house training team coaches students in quantitative aptitude, logical/verbal reasoning, interview skills, and group discussions. Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) has a dynamic Training Department, led by Prof. P. Padmaja, Dean - Training, and supported by a dedicated team of 19 faculty coordinators. The department delivers extensive training in Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, English proficiency, Group Discussions and Interview Skills. Programs are tailored to industry needs and delivered primarily by experienced in-house trainers, ensuring students are well-prepared for recruitment processes.

The department fosters robust connections with industry partners, regularly organizing expert lectures, technical workshops and interactive sessions that expose students to current practices and future trends.

ANITS, with the Training Department at the forefront, remains a top destination for aspiring engineers, empowering students with the right skills, knowledge and opportunities to launch successful careers.

Our Department Focuses On

  • Training Structure: Classes happen during the 3rd year to build strong foundational skills before real job tests begin.

  • Placement Cell Facilities: The campus has a 400-seat air-conditioned auditorium for pre-placement talks, 500-student capacity exam halls, and AC interview rooms.

  • Recent Stats: During the placement season, the highest package offered was up to Rs. 17.08 LPA, with a median package around Rs. 4.03 LPA.

  • Top Recruiters: Students frequently secure jobs at companies like Walmart, Amazon, Infosys, and Flipkart.
